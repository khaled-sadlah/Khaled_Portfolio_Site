Smart Glasses for Blind and Visually Impaired People

An intelligent graduation project designed to assist visually impaired users in indoor university environments using voice assistance, computer vision, and a companion mobile application.

Face Recognition — identify known people in real time.
OCR Text Reading — detect and read printed English text aloud.
Object Detection — recognize surrounding objects and warn the user.
Indoor Navigation — guide users between rooms using Wi-Fi positioning and path planning.
Flutter Companion App — manage faces, maps, QR pairing, and emergency notifications.
